Privacy policy

When you place an order, Nutikals receives the name, phone number, email, delivery address, city, postal code and order note you provide. We use these details to confirm and deliver your order.

Order details are stored by the storefront so the Nutikals team can process the request. The shopping cart is stored in your browser. This checkout does not collect payment card details. If you choose JazzCash, payment is arranged manually with the team.

To ask about, correct or remove your order details, contact us on WhatsApp at +92 330 8348288.
